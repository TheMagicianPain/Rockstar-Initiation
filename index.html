The method

Five stages. One visible transformation.

A personal, tarot-inspired process combining self-discovery, persona design, expressive practice, guided mental rehearsal, and a deliberate public debut.

I

The Fool — Discover

Return to the styles, fascinations, characters, and forms of expression you gravitated toward naturally—before you began editing yourself for other people.

Outcome: Your original expression profile
II

The Persona — Design

Define the look, aesthetic, movement, attitude, and public presence of your next persona. Give it a name if you want; you can always change it.

Outcome: Your personal persona blueprint
III

The Programming — Embody

Set three high-leverage changes: one behavior to stop, one to start, and one change to your environment that supports your new expression.

Outcome: Your three-change embodiment protocol
IV

The Manifesto — Reinforce

Create a personalized guided affirmation and visualization with me in the first session, then revisit your recording daily as creative mental rehearsal.

Outcome: Your personal audio manifesto
V

The Liberation — Reveal

In week three, make a bold, intentional public debut of your chosen look or persona—through fashion, photography, performance, a creative reveal, or another meaningful act.

Outcome: Your liberation debut and integration

Questions

Before you begin.

Do I need to be an artist or musician?

No. This experience is for anyone ready to intentionally reinvent their persona and outward expression, whether that happens in art, fashion, work, social life, or everyday presence.

Do I need to know my new persona already?

No. It can start as a feeling, an image, or a way of carrying yourself. We turn that direction into a personal, practical expression.

What if my persona changes?

That is welcome. Your persona name and aesthetic can evolve. The process is about experimentation and authentic choice, not committing to a permanent mask.

Is this hypnotherapy?

The experience incorporates principles from hypnotherapy through personalized affirmations, guided visualization, and creative mental rehearsal. It is not clinical hypnotherapy, psychotherapy, or treatment for a mental health condition. If you have a mental health condition, seek care from a qualified healthcare professional.

What happens after I apply?

You will receive a tarot-inspired reflective reading, submit your application, and receive a link to book a discovery call to discuss the private experience.

Is enrollment guaranteed?

No. A discovery call is an opportunity to discuss your goals and determine whether working together is a good fit.
